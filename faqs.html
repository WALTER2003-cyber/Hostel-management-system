Knowledge Base & FAQs

Manage common customer queries and platform documentation.

How do I reset a user's password?

To reset a user's password, navigate to the "User Details" tab, search for the specific user, and click on the "Security" panel. From there, you can generate a one-time password reset link that will be emailed directly to them.

What happens when my workspace exceeds its active user limit?

If your workspace goes over the active user allocation for your current billing tier, new sign-ups will be temporarily paused. The system will trigger an automatic notification asking you to upgrade your plan via the Billing tab in Settings.

Can I export system logs and reports?

Yes. Administrators can export audit logs, user session data, and performance reports as CSV or PDF files directly from the Dashboard quick actions or the specialized Reports module.
